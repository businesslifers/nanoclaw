## Brand Positioning

Carpet One is a **co-operative** and one of the world's largest retail floor covering groups, with **over 2700 retail outlets worldwide**. Australian stores are independently owned and operated — local businesses backed by global buying power.

Three core pillars (presented as tabs on homepage):
1. **Global Buying Power** — 2700+ outlets worldwide; best deals negotiated with suppliers, savings passed to customers.
2. **Independent Ownership** — every store is locally owned and operated.
3. **Local Commitment** — community-embedded, locally accountable.

Key CTA phrases:
- "Showroom to You" (mobile showroom — bring samples to your home)
- "Visit us In-Store"
- "Let us give you a quote. You'll be happy you did!"
- "Your Total Price, Sorted" — product + underlay + installation in one clear quote, no surprises.

| **Tone** | Warm, knowledgeable, reassuring, practical. Speaks like a trusted local expert, not a corporate brand. |

## Product Range

### Carpet
Sub-categories: Wool Carpet, Desire Collection, Carpet Tile, Carpet Cleaning, Triexta Carpet, Rugs, Commercial Carpet

**Carpet types explained on site:**
- **Wool** — renewable, soft, resilient, flame-retardant, premium. Temperature and sound regulating.
- **Nylon** — durable, elastic, stain-resistant (especially solution-dyed). Good for heavy traffic.
- **Polyester (PET)** — budget-friendly, suited for low-to-medium traffic (bedrooms).
- **Polypropylene (Olefin)** — moisture/stain-resistant, least expensive, lowest resiliency.
- **Triexta** — newer synthetic, soft + stain resistant + durable. Premium nylon alternative.
- **Tufted** — most common, versatile, budget-friendly.
- **Woven (Wilton/Axminster)** — premium, intricate patterns, up to 20-year lifespan.
- **Pile styles:** Loop, Cut (twist/plush), Cut-and-loop.
- **Broadloom vs Carpet Tiles** — broadloom for continuous comfort, tiles for flexibility.

Popular carpet product names: Ambush, Andes Peak, Astoria, Barossa, Byng, Carramar 4M, City Secrets/Critics Choice, Coonawarra, Crossley Twist, Emmeline, Eureka Tower, Flinders Gorge, Marton, Montrosa, Neighborly, Owensborough, Pacific, Soft Sunset, Spinifex, Vivid Charm

### Hard Flooring
Sub-categories: Luxury Vinyl Plank & Tile, Laminate, Hybrid, Cork, Commercial Flooring, Engineered Timber, Bamboo, Sheet Vinyl, Hard Flooring Cleaning

- **Engineered Timber** — natural grain, warm/classic, more affordable than solid timber.
- **Laminate** — budget-friendly wood look, durable, easy to clean.
- **Vinyl Plank & Tile (LVP)** — water-resistant, versatile, timber/stone looks.
- **Hybrid** — rigid core, water-resistant, stable, easy maintenance.
- **Bamboo** — sustainable/eco-friendly, modern aesthetic, rapidly renewable.
- **Cork** — eco-friendly, thermal/acoustic insulation, cushioned underfoot.
- **Sheet Vinyl** — seamless, easy to clean, spill-resistant.

Popular hard flooring products: Apollo 1500, Arc Bamboo, Argo TX, Australian Hardwood Naturals, Donato, Korlok, Looselay Longboard, Looselay Originals, MiPlank, Noosa TX, NuCore Excellence, Polaris 1500, Primetex 4M, Quick-Step Amato/Impressive Ultra/Majestic/ReadyFlor, Readycork Ambient, ResiPlank Hybrid Summit 977, Tanami, Titan Hybrid XXL, Vista

### Windows
Sub-categories: Blinds, Soft Shades, Shutters, Curtains, Outdoor Awnings

- **Blinds** — Venetian (aluminium/vinyl/faux wood), Vertical
- **Shades** — Roller (light-filtering or blackout), Roman, Cellular/Honeycomb (most energy-efficient)
- **Curtains/Drapes** — lightweight/casual to heavy/lined; motorised options available
- **Shutters** — Plantation shutters popular; timeless, durable, architectural value
- **Outdoor Awnings** — fixed or retractable; also exterior shutters and outdoor blinds

Note: *"many of our stores also offer"* — windows are available at select stores only, not universal.
