## Photos: written recommendations only

- **Logo**: Upload a clean version of the Qld Capital logo on a white or light background. This is the thumbnail that appears in search and Maps.
- **Team photo**: A photo of the lending team or key contacts. Clients and brokers want to know who they are dealing with.
- **Office**: A photo of the Level 3, 82 Eagle Street office interior or reception. Reinforces the Brisbane address.
- **Funded project photos**: Photos of completed developments the business has funded. These need client permission. Even 2 or 3 strong project shots significantly lift the profile.
- **Cover photo**: A strong, professional image at the recommended 1080x608 pixel size. Avoid stock photography. A real project or the office exterior works best.

Once uploaded, set the cover photo to the most visually impressive funded project image.
