Dear Noah,

This proposal covers the redevelopment of the Swimming NSW website onto a single, modern WordPress platform: consolidating your two existing sites, building out the Events and Resource Hub functionality your team needs, and creating a site that's straightforward for your team to manage and update day to day.

**A note on our approach to this estimate**

We've built this scope and estimate from the information available at this point: the brief, the design artwork from your previous developer, and our understanding of the key features required. We're confident in what we've put together, and we want to be transparent about where some details are still to be confirmed.

A handful of scope elements will be finalised during the Blueprint Strategy phase at the start of the project:

- The precise number of additional page templates required (we've estimated 2 to 3 based on what we know now)
- The complete field structure and taxonomy for the Resource Hub
- Final content volume for the content entry allowance

This is standard practice for a project at this stage, and the Blueprint phase exists precisely to nail these details down before development begins. It protects your budget, keeps the build on track, and means there are no surprises once we're underway.

**What we're building on**

The site will be built on WordPress using GreyboxPro, a theme framework designed around ease of editing. Your team will be able to update content, manage events and resources, and add new pages without needing developer involvement.

For the design, we'll be adapting the artwork your previous developer supplied. To streamline the process and reduce costs, we'll use AI to do the initial conversion and refine from there. You'll end up with a full set of production-ready source files for the project.

We've also included options below the main estimate for migrating your existing Drupal content to WordPress, which are worth discussing once you've had a chance to review the core scope.

We're ready to start and happy to talk through any aspect of this proposal.
