# Email Platform Comparison

_Concept: Tool Selection_

## Context
Evaluated April 2026 for Mettro's email automation needs. Core requirement: email automation only, no CRM bundled in, Make.com integration, multi-step sequences. Raels has used ActiveCampaign (liked email, not the pipeline), Kit (okay), and Mailchimp (not right). Decision pending.

## Platforms Compared

### Brevo
- Pricing: per email sent (not per contact) — flat as list grows
- Free plan: 300 emails/day, unlimited contacts
- ~$18/mo (Starter) at any list size up to moderate volume
- Has CRM module but entirely optional
- Make.com native integration confirmed
- Transactional email included
- Best long-term value if list scales to 5k+

### MailerLite
- Pricing: per subscriber
- Free to 500 subs with basic automation
- ~$35/mo at 2–3k contacts
- No CRM at all — pure email
- Cleanest interface, easiest to set up
- Make.com native integration confirmed
- Gets less competitive vs Brevo above 2–3k subs

### Kit (formerly ConvertKit)
- Pricing: per subscriber
- Free to 10,000 subs (1 automation only on free)
- ~A$45/mo (Creator) at 1k subs for full automation
- No CRM bundled
- Built for content creators
- Make.com integration needs verifying

### ActiveCampaign
- No free plan
- Starter caps automation at 5 actions — unusable for real sequences
- Need Plus at ~A$56/mo minimum
- CRM baked in and can't be removed
- Most powerful but most complex and most expensive
- **Ruled out** — CRM can't be removed, Starter plan too limited

## Recommendation
- **MailerLite** — if priority is getting started quickly and cheaply (under 2k contacts)
- **Brevo** — if priority is flat pricing long-term as list grows
