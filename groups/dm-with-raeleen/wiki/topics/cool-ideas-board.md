## Overview
A ClickUp list for saving interesting tools, websites, and ideas for future reference or client use.

**List ID:** `901601631257`
**Location:** Operations > Admin
**URL:** https://app.clickup.com/9003245964/v/li/901601631257

## When adding a task
When Raels drops a URL or idea, create a task with:
1. **Name** — descriptive title (tool name + what it does in brief)
2. **Description** — 2–3 sentence summary of what it is and why it's interesting
3. **URL field** — the link
4. **Labels** — pick the most relevant from the list below
