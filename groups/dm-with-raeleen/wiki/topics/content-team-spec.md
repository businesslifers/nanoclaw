## Content Types

- Blog posts
- Website content (landing pages, service pages, product pages)
- Social content — **OUT OF SCOPE** (PostMate/separate channel)

## Content Standards
- Written for SEO / AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) / GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation)
- Article writing spec to come from Derek's docs (pending)
- Style: first-person plural, warm/human/collaborative, short paragraphs

## Case Study Structure (Mettro format — updated May 2026)
1. Opening Summary (2–3 sentences, leads with the result — AEO extraction point)
2. The Problem
3. The Approach
4. The Result

No bullet points. No "Why It Worked" section. Outcome-first framing throughout. Client is the subject — not Mettro.

Full writing standards in the [Mettro Case Study Brief](https://app.clickup.com/9003245964/v/dc/8ca58cc-93956/8ca58cc-23616) and [Case Study Writing SOP](https://app.clickup.com/t/86d2vamrn).

## WordPress Publishing (Full Metadata)
- Title tag, meta description, slug
- Featured image + alt text
- Categories, tags, author
- Schema markup (for AEO/GEO)
- Internal links
- Open Graph tags
