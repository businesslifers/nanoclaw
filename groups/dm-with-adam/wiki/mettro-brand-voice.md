# Mettro Brand Voice & Copywriting Guidelines

Last updated: 2026-05-01

Guidelines for writing Mettro website copy, marketing content, and client-facing materials. Learned through Raels' copy review sessions (Apr–May 2026).

**Client industries served:** Startups, national brands, government bodies, healthcare providers, property developers, financial sector. Avoid claiming "every industry" — that's an overstatement.

## Voice and Tone

- Confident, direct, and warm — not agency-fluff or tech-speak
- A little personality and quirk is welcome (Raels likes unexpected brackets or asides)
- Write for humans, not for search engines or other agencies
- Avoid grandiose claims — be specific and honest

## Specific Rules (Raels-confirmed)

| Rule | Notes |
|------|-------|
| No em dashes | Standing rule across all Mettro writing |
| No colons in body copy | Flagged Apr 30 — restructure the sentence instead |
| Don't say "users" | Say "customers" or "people" or the specific audience |
| Don't knock other agencies | "Let's not poo poo other agencies" — Apr 30 |
| Don't overuse "we" and "our" | A marketing-experienced client flagged this Apr 30. Where possible, shift to 2nd person ("you", "your") to focus on the reader |
| Don't claim to be a partner | Don't say "we see ourselves as a partner" — too presumptuous. Foster the relationship, don't assume it |
| Don't say "we see ourselves as just a supplier" either | Mettro IS a supplier people call when things break — that's fine. Don't downplay it |
| Include AEO/GEO alongside SEO | When listing digital marketing services, mention answer engine optimisation and GEO (generative engine optimisation) — not just "SEO" |
| "foster" not "invest" in relationships | "We foster long-term relationships" reads better than "we invest in relationships" |
| Mettro is a support option too | They work with clients who just want support/maintenance, not just full builds — "a rebuild, targeted improvements, or everything in between" |

## What Mettro Is Not

Avoid these phrasings — they've been flagged as wrong or clumsy:
- "Not just a supplier you call when something breaks" — they ARE that supplier and that's not a bad thing
- "We see ourselves as partners" — too assumptive
- "We get our team of developers and designers to..." — say "our senior team" instead
- "every industry and every business size" — overstates the truth
- "The one most agencies skip. We don't." — Raels liked this opening line, keep it

## Structural Rules (Website Copy)

- **Breadcrumbs on every page** — flagged by Raels Apr 30; designer must be briefed on this
- **Heading hierarchy matters** — Raels cares about H1/H2/H3 structure; always include heading levels when writing for ClickUp or docs
- **Don't write "bitsy" sections** — Raels flags copy that breaks into too many tiny headed sections as "bitsy" — consider flowing copy with minimal subheads when appropriate
- **Intro paragraphs must work hard** — weak intros get flagged ("this is a little weak as an intro", "this one is a bit of nothing")
- Services page: clearly lead with 3 main areas (Website Design & Development, Digital Marketing, Support & Maintenance) before sub-services

## Mettro's Services Description Approach

Brief descriptions for each area that Raels approved (as of Apr 30):

**Support & Maintenance:** "The one most agencies skip. We don't." — then talk about: updates, staying ahead, keeping everything running smoothly, 20-year client relationships, showing up when it counts (not just at launch). Don't lead with "websites going down and getting hacked" — it's bigger than that.

**Digital Marketing:** Mention Google Ads, Meta Ads, SEO/AEO/GEO, email marketing, social media, full sales funnels. "We manage every dollar like it's our own" — weave this into the paragraph rather than tacking it on as a final line.

**Website Design & Development:** Lead with strategy before pixel placement. "Who are your customers, what do they need, and how does the site perform long after go-live?" — not "users", "customers". Mention WordPress, custom builds, and GreyboxPro.
