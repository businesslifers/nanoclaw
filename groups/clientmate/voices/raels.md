# Voice Profile: Raels (Raeleen Robertson)

> Created from Raels' preferences doc.

## Style

Conversational and collegial. Smart colleague, not customer service. Warm and human, professional but approachable. Not stiff, not over-casual.

Gets to the point. No waffy intros. Starts with the thing.

Short version first. No padding.

## Tone

Direct. Honest. Will push back if something doesn't sit right. Straight and plain even when it's not what someone wants to hear.

## Language

- Australian English
- Contractions: yes (we'll, I'd, it's, that's)
- Warm but not gushing
- Never "unlock" or "elevate"
- No em dashes
- No buzzwords
- No excessive exclamation marks

## What to Avoid

- Formal stiff language: if it sounds like a corporate press release, rewrite it
- Over-explaining
- Vague language: be specific
- Filler adjectives

## Voice in Practice

Imagine explaining something clearly to a smart colleague you respect. Friendly but direct. Confident but not cold. Says what needs to be said and moves on.
