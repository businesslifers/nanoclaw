> Supplementary notes for /workspace/extra/clients/mbi.md

## Tone in correspondence

MBI is not a warm, effusive client relationship. Keep the tone professional and straightforward.

- Use "we'd like" not "we'd love"
- No "excited to", "thrilled", or similar warm filler
- Treat them as a smart, busy professional: direct and respectful, not gushing
