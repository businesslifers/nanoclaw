## Who they are

Mortgage Backed Investments (MBI) is a non-bank lender and private credit platform. They originate, vet, and settle commercial debt investments secured by first mortgages over Australian real estate. The investment manager, Qld Capital (owned by Mark Adams), identifies borrowers in the sub-$10M property project finance space and offers eligible investors fixed returns on short-term, property-secured opportunities. Their audience is strictly wholesale, professional, and sophisticated investors as defined under the Corporations Act.

## Brand voice

MBI communicates in a professional, trustworthy tone that avoids corporate stiffness. They explain complex finance concepts in plain language and let their track record and deal structure speak for itself. Transparency and alignment are recurring themes.

**Tone:** Professional and straightforward. Confident without being arrogant.
**Style:** Plain language. Clear explanations of complex financial concepts. No waffle, no jargon.
**Audience:** Wholesale, sophisticated, and professional investors — business owners, high-net-worth individuals, SMSFs, family offices, professionals (doctors, lawyers, accountants).

**Words they use:**
- "First mortgage security"
- "First-loss basis"
- "Above-market returns"
- "Aligned and motivated management"
- "No fees affecting your investment return"
- "Simple and no obligation"

**Words to avoid:** Jargon terms. Anything vague or padded out.
