# Arrow Energy

## Overview

**Website:** https://www.arrowenergy.com.au/
**Staging:** https://arrowenergystg.wpenginepowered.com/
**ABN:** 73 078 521 936
**Type:** Queensland-based natural gas and electricity producer
**Ownership:** 50/50 joint venture between Shell and PetroChina (since 2009)

Arrow Energy is a Queensland-based producer of natural gas and electricity, operating in the Surat and Bowen basins. They have a 20+ year track record, with their first well drilled in 2001. They work on agricultural land in partnership with farming families.

**Tagline / Vision:** "Stronger Together" / "Be the most competitive and respected developer of unconventional gas value chains in Australia"

**Mission:** Deliver more gas to create shared value and power the energy transition.
