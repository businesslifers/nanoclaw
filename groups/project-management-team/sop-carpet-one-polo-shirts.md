## Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1 — Receive Enquiry
- Store contacts Mettro to enquire about polo shirts.
- Note: stores can also self-serve via The Hub (link above) for images and general info.

### Step 2 — Send Initial Quote
- Reply with the client-facing quote template above.
- Attach the sizing chart (POLO SIZE CHART.png) and shirt images.
- Ask the store to confirm:
  - **Total quantity** required
  - **Style and size** for each person (Adult / Lady / Lady Relax / Junior + size)

### Step 3 — Collect Size Breakdown
- Obtain the complete size list before progressing.
- Do not proceed to confirmed quote until all sizes and quantities are confirmed.

### Step 4 — Get Confirmed Quote from Fully Promoted
- Contact **Monica at Fully Promoted (The Gabba)** with the confirmed quantity and size breakdown.
- Request a confirmed per-unit cost and delivery quote.
- Do not share any client details with the supplier beyond what is needed for the quote.

### Step 5 — Issue Confirmed Quote to Store
- Send the store a confirmed quote at the **marked-up client price** (based on Step 4 confirmed costs).
- Include delivery cost as a separate line item.
- Request written approval from the store to proceed.

### Step 6 — Invoice the Store
- Once the store approves the quote, raise an invoice for the **full amount** (shirts + delivery).
- **Payment must be received in full before any order is placed with Fully Promoted.**

### Step 7 — Place Order with Fully Promoted
- Only after payment is confirmed, contact Monica at Fully Promoted to place the order.
- Provide: confirmed size breakdown, quantity, delivery address, and any relevant reference.

### Step 8 — Monitor & Communicate Lead Times
- Remind the store of lead times: **3–5 weeks production + 3–7 days dispatch**.
- Follow up with Monica if no dispatch confirmation is received within 4 weeks.
- Notify the store when order has been dispatched and provide tracking if available.
