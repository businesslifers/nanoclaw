**Janet**: <message to="slack-mg-17785">In Zoho CRM, there are a couple of ways to check which campaign emails have been sent to a lead:

**From the Lead record:**
1. Open the lead's record
2. Scroll down to the **Related Lists** section
3. Look for **Campaigns** — this shows any campaigns the lead has been added to as a member

**To see the specific emails sent:**
1. In the lead record, check the **Emails** section (usually in the timeline/activity area) — this shows individual emails that were sent
2. You can also look at the **Activities** timeline for a chronological view

**From the Campaigns module:**
1. Go to **Campaigns**
2. Open the relevant campaign
3. Click on **Campaign Members** to find the lead and their status (Sent, Opened, Clicked, etc.)

The "Campaigns" related list on the lead record is usually the quickest way to see all campaigns they've been associated with at a glance.

Note: the exact layout can vary depending on your Zoho CRM version and how your admin has set up the page layouts. If you're not seeing a Campaigns section on the lead record, it may need to be enabled by your CRM admin.</message>

**Janet**: <message to="slack-mg-17785">Yes, Zoho CRM does track opens and clicks — here's how to access that and search for leads with campaign activity:

**Viewing open/click status on a lead:**
1. Go to the **Campaigns** module
2. Open the campaign
3. Click **Campaign Members**
4. You'll see a status column showing each lead's activity — typically: Sent, Opened, Clicked, Bounced, Unsubscribed

**Searching/filtering leads by campaign activity:**

*Option 1 — Filter within Campaign Members:*
- Inside a campaign's Members list, you can filter by status (e.g. show only "Clicked" or "Opened")

*Option 2 — Create a Report:*
1. Go to **Reports** → **Create Report**
2. Choose **Campaigns** or **Leads** as the module
3. Add filters for campaign status (Opened, Clicked, etc.)
4. This gives you a reusable, searchable list

*Option 3 — Advanced Filter on Leads module:*
1. Go to **Leads**
2. Use **Advanced Filter** (funnel icon)
3. Look for campaign-related fields to filter by

**Important caveat:** The depth of tracking (especially clicks vs. just opens) can depend on:
- Whether you're using **Zoho CRM's built-in campaigns** or the separate **Zoho Campaigns** app
- If using **Zoho Campaigns**, the tracking is much more detailed and syncs back to CRM contact/lead records

Which are you using — the built-in CRM campaigns or Zoho Campaigns as a separate tool?</message>
