# research-local-market — intent statement

**Requirement (verbatim):** Reviews a store's local market area to identify marketing opportunities. This may include local suburbs, nearby developments, competitor activity, demographic trends, community groups, sponsorship opportunities, local search visibility and potential campaign ideas suited to that store's location.

**Analysis context:** This mate was scoped after determining the requirement could not be an extension to `research-opportunities`. The research-opportunities mate explicitly hands off local search visibility (to an SEO collaborator), formal demographic research (to a research collaborator), and has no coverage of sponsorship opportunities or community groups. The two mates work from opposite primary lenses: research-opportunities starts from online VOC signal; this mate starts from a store's physical trading area and derives marketing opportunities from that spatial, demographic, and community context.

**Role scope:** A local market analyst role for brick-and-mortar or service-area businesses. Scans the physical catchment area and its population, competitive, and community fabric to produce a prioritised set of location-specific marketing opportunities. Upstream of campaigns — output is intelligence and opportunity sets that feed into planning, not running media or managing content.

**Distinct capability areas:**
1. Catchment area demographics — suburb profiles, household composition, income tiers, growth/decline trajectory, sourced from publicly available census and council data
2. Nearby developments — new residential, commercial, or infrastructure projects that shift the trading population or introduce new competitors
3. Competitor presence mapping — who is operating in the area, how they are positioned, and where they are weak or absent
4. Community groups and organisations — clubs, schools, associations, local Facebook groups, noticeboards, events
5. Sponsorship opportunities — local teams, events, causes, and organisations suited to the store's category and audience
6. Local search visibility — Google Business Profile presence and optimisation state, local pack rankings, map listings for the store and key competitors
7. Location-specific campaign ideas — opportunities tied to the demographic, community, or competitive landscape of this specific store's area

**Kingdom:** `research` (pre-production intelligence, upstream of campaigns; all `marketing-*` mates are paid-media platforms, not research functions)
