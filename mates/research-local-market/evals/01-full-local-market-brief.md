# Full local market brief for a new café

## Task

A café is opening in Fitzroy North, Melbourne. The store is on Scotchmer Street, seats 40 inside and 20 outside, and targets working locals and young families. Produce a full local market brief.

## Conditions to compare

1. Baseline model (no mate context)
2. Mate prompt only (no wiki, no references)
3. Mate prompt + wiki
4. Mate prompt + wiki + project context (store address, menu focus, price point, opening date)

## What good looks like

- Opens with a one-paragraph catchment summary naming Fitzroy North plus the adjacent suburbs in the default walkable radius, with a one-line demographic character read (e.g. inner-urban renters and owner-occupiers, median age range, household income band)
- Three to five opportunities, each in the five-part format (location signal, evidence, gap, deployment path, confidence)
- At least one opportunity tied to a specific local development or demographic trend (not generic café advice)
- Community group section naming at least three real or plausible local surfaces (neighbourhood Facebook groups, school P&C associations, local running clubs, etc.) with platform, reach estimate, and audience-fit verdict
- At least two sponsorship findings with audience and fit reason specific to a café in this suburb
- Local search summary stating GBP status, at least three category + suburb query combinations checked, and one specific gap or competitor finding
- Deployment paths are directional and café-specific — not "run social ads" but "an organic Instagram series targeting local parents around the school-run window"
- Confidence levels stated per opportunity

## What bad looks like

- Generic café marketing advice that could apply to any suburb in any city
- Demographic claims without a source type or `[estimate]` tag
- Opportunities that describe what the store already is ("your outdoor seating is an opportunity") rather than gaps in the local market
- Community section with made-up or unverifiable group names presented as confirmed
- Local search summary with no specific queries named or no competitor finding
- Missing five-part structure on any opportunity
- Deployment paths that are actually campaign briefs (too long, too tactical)

## Notes

Fitzroy North is a real Melbourne suburb with publicly available demographic data. A strong response will reflect the actual demographic character of the suburb (inner-urban, above-average income, high proportion of young families and professionals) rather than inventing a generic profile. Graders should check whether the catchment extension to adjacent suburbs (Northcote, Brunswick, Clifton Hill) is reasonable for a walkable café catchment.
