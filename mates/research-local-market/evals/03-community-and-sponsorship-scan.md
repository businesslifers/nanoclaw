# Community and sponsorship scan for a children's activity centre

## Task

A children's indoor activity and party venue is located in Robina, Gold Coast. The business wants to identify community groups they should be present in and sponsorship opportunities within the local area. Produce a community and sponsorship scan only — no competitor analysis, no local search assessment.

## Conditions to compare

1. Baseline model (no mate context)
2. Mate prompt only (no wiki, no references)
3. Mate prompt + wiki
4. Mate prompt + wiki + project context (age range served: 2–12 years, party bookings as the primary revenue driver, current absence from all community surfaces)

## What good looks like

- Community groups section: at least four named community surfaces with platform, approximate reach or member count where visible, and a one-line audience-fit verdict specific to a children's venue
  - Surfaces should span different types: at minimum one suburb/local parenting Facebook group, one school-adjacent surface (P&C, school newsletter), and one organised children's activity (sports club, dance school, swimming club)
  - Audience-fit verdict is specific: "high fit — majority of members are parents of children aged 4–10" is better than "suitable audience"
- Sponsorship section: at least two sponsorship opportunities with the opportunity named, audience reach and composition stated, fit reason tied to a children's activity venue, and indicative involvement form
  - At least one sponsorship finding involves a local children's sporting or activity competition (not just a general community event)
  - Fit reason names why this specific venue type (indoor activity + parties) benefits from this sponsorship — not just "reaches families"
- Low-fit findings are noted rather than omitted — if a community group reaches the right suburb but the wrong demographic, it is flagged as low-fit rather than excluded silently
- Output declares itself as a standalone community and sponsorship scan (not attempting to produce a full market brief)

## What bad looks like

- Generic "sponsor a local sports team" advice without naming a specific team, league, or event type in Robina or the Gold Coast
- Community groups that are plausible nationally but not confirmed as active locally (e.g. "there will be Facebook groups for Robina parents" without naming or verifying one)
- All four community surfaces are the same type (e.g. four Facebook groups)
- Sponsorship opportunities that reach adults without children (e.g. a local business awards dinner)
- Audience-fit verdict is the same generic phrase on every entry
- Missing platform or reach information for community groups

## Notes

Robina is a real Gold Coast suburb with significant residential family population, the Robina Town Centre, and multiple schools. A strong response reflects the family-oriented demographic character of the suburb. Graders should check whether community surfaces named are plausible for the Robina/Gold Coast area even if not individually verifiable without live web access.
